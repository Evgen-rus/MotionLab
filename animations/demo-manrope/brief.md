# Demo Manrope

Same text, duration (2 s), 24 fps and movement as animations/demo. Typography: Manrope 800 heading/tag, Manrope 500 explanations/cards. Purple-to-blue accent gradient. No terminal/code scenes, so no monospace type.

Font: official Google Fonts ofl/manrope/Manrope[wght].ttf, downloaded 2026-10-31, unmodified, bundled locally. License: fonts/OFL.txt. Not installed system-wide. The scene explicitly waits for both font weights and rejects a missing font.
